# Official IFC documents to download

These are IFC publications. They are not included in this package: download them yourself so you always have the current versions. Save them with clear file names, because Claude refers to files more reliably when names are descriptive.

| Document | Where | Suggested file name | Needed for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Performance Standards on Environmental and Social Sustainability (2012) | https://www.ifc.org/performancestandards (full document PDF: https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/mgrt/ps-english-2012-full-document.pdf) | IFC_PS_2012.pdf | Core requirements: every screening |
| Guidance Notes to the Performance Standards (2012, updated 14 June 2021) | https://www.ifc.org/performancestandards (look under Guidance Notes) | IFC_GN_2012_rev2021.pdf | Explains each requirement; the GN paragraph citations |
| IFC Policy on Environmental and Social Sustainability (1 January 2012) | https://www.ifc.org/content/dam/ifc/doc/mgrt/sp-english-2012.pdf (page: https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2012/publications-policy-sustainability-2012) | IFC_Sustainability_Policy_2012.pdf | Categories A, B, C, FI-1 to FI-3 (para 40) and how to categorise (paras 41-44) |
| IFC Guidance Note on Financial Intermediaries (29 September 2023; replaces the 2018 Interpretation Note) | https://www.ifc.org/en/insights-reports/2012/publications-policy-interpretationnote-fi | IFC_GN_FI_2023.pdf | Screening banks, NBFCs, funds and insurers |
| IFC Exclusion List (2007, amended 8 December 2025) | https://www.ifc.org/exclusionlist | IFC_Exclusion_List.pdf (print the page to PDF) | Activities IFC does not finance; the extra FI exclusions |
| World Bank Group General EHS Guidelines, plus the sector guideline for your projects | https://www.ifc.org/ehsguidelines | WBG_EHS_General.pdf, plus one per sector | Performance levels that PS2, PS3 and PS4 refer to |
| National law note (optional) | Your own, or one from EMC | | For India: EIA Notification 2006, RFCTLARR 2013, Forest Rights Act 2006, AMASR Act 1958 |

Notes
- If a link has moved, start from https://www.ifc.org/performancestandards and look for the document by title.
- The Exclusion List was amended on 8 December 2025 to remove "production or trade in radioactive materials". An older copy that still lists it is out of date.
- IFC is reviewing its Sustainability Framework. Dialogue runs to December 2026 and public consultation on draft proposals starts in January 2027. Nothing is approved and no effective date is set. The 2012 documents remain in force.
- Where to use them: upload them to the optional Claude Project (see 3_Optional_Claude_Project) so Claude can quote exact paragraph text. The checklist and the Skill work without them.
