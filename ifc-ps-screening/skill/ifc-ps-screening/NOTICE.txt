IFC PS Screening skill, version 1.2 (October 2026)
Developed by Environmental Management Centre (EMC), Mumbai, for participants of
"Both Sides of the Table: Masterclass on IFC Performance Standards" (Ekonnect Knowledge Foundation).

Provided for the professional use of masterclass participants. Not for resale or
redistribution without EMC's written permission.

This skill supports first-pass screening only. It does not replace an ESDD, ESIA or ESMS.
The IFC Performance Standards and Guidance Notes are IFC publications; download them from ifc.org.
Bundled rule set: EMC IFC PS screening rules v1.2.0 (references/ifc_ps_rules.json), the same rule set as the EMC checklist.
Performance Standard paragraph numbers were checked against the 2012 text (Guidance Notes updated June 2021).
Category meanings follow the IFC Sustainability Policy (2012) and the FI rules follow the IFC Guidance Note on Financial Intermediaries (September 2023). Sector risk classes, the answers-to-category rules and the sanity checks are EMC judgement.
The IFC Exclusion List text was supplied by EMC from ifc.org (amended 8 December 2025).
India-law content reflects general knowledge as of October 2026 and must be verified.
