# Financial intermediaries (FI): banks, NBFCs, funds, insurers

Sources: IFC Policy on Environmental and Social Sustainability (1 January 2012), paragraphs 32-37, 40 and 44; IFC Guidance Note on Financial Intermediaries (29 September 2023, which replaces the Interpretation Note on FIs of November 2018). Paragraph numbers below are those documents' own. The engine returns category `FI` for sector `fi` and adds the FI actions to PS1; this file is the depth behind them.

## What is different about an FI

The Performance Standards apply to the FI in two ways: to the FI itself (its own staff and premises), and, through the FI's ESMS, to the sub-projects it finances. The FI is responsible for E&S risk management of its borrowers and investees; IFC reviews the FI's capacity and system (Policy paras 33-34, FI GN para 5).

## Sub-categories (Policy para 40; FI GN para 16)

IFC sets FI-1, FI-2 or FI-3 from the type, size and sector exposure of the portfolio financed by IFC proceeds, including tenor (Policy para 44).

| | When | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| FI-1 | Existing or proposed portfolio includes substantial exposure to activities with potential significant adverse impacts that are diverse, irreversible or unprecedented | ESMS; Performance Standards applied to Higher Risk Transactions; annual report to IFC; incident notification |
| FI-2 | Portfolio is business activities with limited, site-specific, largely reversible impacts; or includes a very limited number of significant-impact activities | ESMS; Performance Standards applied to any Higher Risk Transactions it supports; annual report; incident notification |
| FI-3 | Portfolio predominantly minimal or no adverse impacts | Exclusion List and national law screening; a simple screening procedure counts as the ESMS (FI GN fn 20); IFC monitors only changes in the portfolio |

The checklist does not collect the portfolio, so the engine cannot choose between FI-1, FI-2 and FI-3. Ask for the sector and product breakdown of the portfolio to be financed (including exposure to coal, palm oil and other high-risk activities), or say the sub-category is open.

## What every FI must do (Policy para 35; FI GN paras 6-8, 17-18)

- Develop and operate an ESMS that fits the risk of the portfolio and incorporates the relevant principles of PS1: E&S policy; organisational capacity; identification of risks and ESDD of each transaction; management programmes and ESAPs; monitoring and review; external communications mechanism; emergency preparedness (FI GN para 23). Some FI types with no E&S risk, such as individual retail finance, mortgages and student loans, need no ESMS (FI GN fn 8).
- Apply the IFC Exclusion List, plus the extra FI exclusions (see exclusion-list.md), and national E&S law. At least require borrowers to meet applicable laws, permits and licences (FI GN paras 7, 34). Retail-focused FIs are generally not required to apply the Exclusion List (FI GN fn 10, 31).
- Apply relevant aspects of PS2 to the FI's own employees. PS2 paras 7, 11, 12, 27, 28 and 29 are unlikely to apply to an FI, subject to its own assessment (FI GN para 18). Where the public can enter FI buildings, manage life and fire safety to the WBG EHS General Guidelines section 3.3.
- Review the E&S operations of borrowers and investees where they present risk (FI GN para 8).

## Higher Risk Transactions (FI GN para 9 and fn 13)

For FI-1 and FI-2, the sub-projects that would be Category A or B if IFC financed them directly, involving any of these, must apply the relevant Performance Standards: (i) involuntary resettlement, physical or economic; (ii) risk of adverse impacts on Indigenous Peoples; (iii) significant risks to the environment, community health and safety, biodiversity or cultural heritage; (iv) significant retrenchment; (v) significant occupational health and safety risks. They typically are equity, project finance or long-term (over 36 months) corporate finance. Table 1 of the FI GN (para 17) lists, by product type, what the FI and what the borrower or investee must assess.

The checklist's 33 questions are a ready screen for such a sub-project: use the engine on a representative or the riskiest sub-project, and say so. Where the FI has little leverage (syndicated loans, listed bonds), it judges the likelihood that the sub-project meets the Performance Standards from public information, and should not participate if that likelihood is low (FI GN Table 1 and fn 21).

## Scope of application (Policy paras 36-37; FI GN paras 3-4)

- Specified end use (for example a credit line for SMEs): covers that asset class only, and also the same asset class when the FI finances it from its own account, from the time of IFC's investment.
- Equity or general-purpose support: the whole portfolio originated from the date of IFC's investment (counted from disbursement).
- Existing portfolio before IFC's investment is not covered (FI GN fn 5, 7).
- Double intermediation: normally limited to low and medium-low risk FIs; where allowed, the E&S requirements cascade to the FI's sub-clients (FI GN para 10 and fn 34).

## Reporting and disclosure where IFC is the investor

- Annual E&S performance report to IFC, except FI-3 (FI GN para 52); prompt notification of serious incidents (FI-1 and FI-2, para 54); notification of Higher Risk Transactions.
- A public summary of the ESMS (para 53).
- Private equity funds disclose sub-projects supported with IFC funding other than Category C; commercial banks with new IFC equity or senior debt disclose Category A sub-projects (and certain climate-related Category B sub-projects) (paras 61-66).

## Other restrictions

IFC may restrict other activities in the legal documents. For FI transactions with a defined use of proceeds, coal-related sub-projects (coal mining, coal transport, coal-fired power plants and dedicated infrastructure) are excluded, as is trade finance for thermal coal; for funds, coal is excluded up front or IFC opts out (FI GN para 7, fn 11).

## How to screen an FI deal

1. Run the engine with sector `fi`; read the category line and PS1 actions.
2. Check the Exclusion List and the FI additions against the stated portfolio (exclusion-list.md). Apply the "substantially involved" test where the FI GN gives one.
3. Establish the use of proceeds (specified or general) and so the scope.
4. Ask for the portfolio breakdown and propose FI-1, FI-2 or FI-3 as provisional, labelled "Skill review" if you go beyond the engine, with the facts that would change it.
5. Screen the main Higher Risk Transaction types in the pipeline with the checklist cases.
6. Ask for the existing ESMS and E&S policy and gap-check them against FI GN paras 23-31 (gap check template).
