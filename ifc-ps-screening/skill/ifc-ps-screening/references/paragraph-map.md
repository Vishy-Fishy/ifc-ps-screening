## Guidance Notes used in this skill
- GN2-32: IFC/EBRD guidance on workers' accommodation. GN2-71 to GN2-73: identity documents, unpayable debt, freedom of movement.
- GN4-18 to GN4-20: communicable disease, including with labour influx.
- The Guidance Notes number their notes continuously within each standard (GN1, GN2 ...). The notes cross-refer to the paragraph they explain.
