# India: regional red flags for screening

Use these to decide NEEDS REVIEW or TRIGGERED when the user has not mentioned the issue. They are prompts to check, not findings. Verify against current sources (IBAT, state forest department, census, court orders). Court positions in particular change; where a point below depends on a court order, say which order and date you relied on.

## Biodiversity (PS6)
- **Great Indian Bustard**: priority and potential habitat in western Rajasthan (for example Jaisalmer, Barmer, parts of Jodhpur and Bikaner) and Kutch, Gujarat. Overhead transmission lines are the documented mortality threat. The Supreme Court has heard the issue since 2021 and its directions have changed more than once. A judgment of 19 December 2025 is reported (Down To Earth; the judgment text itself was not reviewed) to have set priority areas of about 14,013 sq km in Rajasthan and 740 sq km in Gujarat; to bar new wind turbines and solar parks above 2 MW, and expansion of existing renewable projects, inside them; to reject blanket undergrounding of power lines in favour of undergrounding or rerouting specified lines; and to set Gujarat timelines running to 2028. Treat any solar, wind or line project in western Rajasthan or Kutch as needing the current judgment text and the notified zone maps before a verdict is final, a species check, and legal advice. Likely critical habitat.
- **Lesser Florican, other grassland birds**: grasslands of Rajasthan, Gujarat, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra. Grassland is natural habitat even when classed as wasteland.
- **Vultures, migratory raptors and waterbirds**: wind and transmission projects on flyways, wetlands and coasts (Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha).
- **Elephant corridors**: Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, West Bengal, Assam, Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu. Linear projects and mines fragment corridors; human-elephant conflict also raises PS4.
- **Western Ghats and Eastern Himalaya**: high endemism; ecologically sensitive area proposals.
- **Himalayan rivers**: hydropower affects fish (including mahseer), flows and slopes; cumulative impacts across cascades; glacial lake outburst and landslide risk (PS4).
- **Coast**: CRZ, mangroves, coral, turtle nesting (Odisha coast), fishing livelihoods (PS5 economic displacement).

## Indigenous Peoples (PS7)
- **Fifth Schedule (Scheduled Areas)**: parts of Andhra Pradesh, Chhattisgarh, Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan and Telangana. Check village-level notification.
- **Sixth Schedule (tribal areas)**: parts of Assam, Meghalaya, Mizoram and Tripura, governed by autonomous district councils.
- Scheduled Tribe communities also live outside these areas; check census data and forest rights claims.

## Land (PS5)
- State solar parks and industrial estates: land often acquired or allotted by state agencies; legacy review needed.
- Revenue and gauchar land in Rajasthan, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh is commonly grazed.
- Transmission right-of-way: crop and tree compensation, tower footprints, restrictions under the line corridor.

## Water and climate (PS3, PS4)
- Water stress and groundwater restrictions: Rajasthan, Gujarat, Haryana, Punjab, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, Telangana and drought-prone Maharashtra. Check CGWA groundwater category of the block.
- Heat stress for outdoor workers (PS2 OHS) across northern and central India in summer; flood and cyclone exposure on the east coast.

## Labour and supply chains (PS2)
- Solar modules: polysilicon and wafer supply chains with forced labour allegations (Xinjiang); ask for traceability.
- Mica (Jharkhand, Bihar), brick kilns (bonded labour), cottonseed and cotton (child labour), stone quarrying, seafood processing, garment subcontracting.
- Migrant construction labour: recruitment through labour contractors, wage deductions, camp conditions.
